ABOUT THE ENGINE

Why Pre-Retrieval Permission Filtering Matters

Standard RAG pipelines retrieve document chunks based purely on vector similarity. In an enterprise, this leads to severe data leakage — employees receiving AI answers generated from restricted HR salaries or internal engineering keys. Our engine filters vector access BEFORE context reaches the LLM.

🔐

Zero Context Leakage

Unauthorized document vectors are omitted prior to similarity search. Restricted information never enters the prompt buffer.

🏢

Department Isolation

Documents are tagged by HR, Engineering, Marketing, and Finance. Users query only what their department clearance permits.

📜

Verifiable Citations

Every generated response includes explicit document source citations, chunk IDs, and confidence scores for compliance auditing.
